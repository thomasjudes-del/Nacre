## Véhicule

Use case: stylized-concept. Production bitmap sprite for NACRE, a melancholic cinematic side-scrolling game set in a drowned botanical conservatory. One entire original maintenance convoy vehicle, isolated on truly transparent background, wide 1536x1024 canvas, takes most width. Strict left side elevation, front facing RIGHT, no perspective foreshortening, both rear and front visible. Low elongated enclosed industrial crawler with six partly concealed small dark wheels, long curved oxidized teal copper roof, three tall frosted glass specimen chambers with dim pearl silhouettes of suspended plants inside, short rounded pilot cabin at right, one tiny amber headlamp on right bumper. Dark desaturated petrol green metal, worn ivory mineral stains, black rubber, modest brass trim, handcrafted credible industrial detailing, moss at seams. Painterly realistic fine textured concept art, cinematic subtle cool rim light and small warm cabin window, no baked light beams, no shadow ground, no surroundings, no humans, no text, no lettering, no white outline. Silhouette slightly unsettling, long botanical hearse, not a cute cartoon. Enough brightness for silhouette to read on dark teal scenery. Cutout alpha, vehicle horizontal with wheel contact points at same y. Keep machine coherent, asymmetrical believable engineering, avoid overcomplicated steampunk.
